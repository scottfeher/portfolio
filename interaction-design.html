Interaction Design

Fortifone — gamified iOS prototype

In this project I created a gamified prototype version of an iOS application aimed at limiting phone usage to help users battle procrastination habits. The brief was a case-study scenario to prototype a digital product that solves a societal issue.

Design intent

Many digital wellbeing tools rely on strict blocking, which users often disable. Fortifone explores a more motivating approach: encouraging behaviour change through gamification and clear feedback, while keeping the user (somewhat) in control.
