Good Things

Social Media Strategy + UX Analysis

During university I worked in a team producing work for a sustainable gift company named Good Things. Our work was split across two terms: term 1 focused on an Instagram content strategy to boost engagement and brand awareness; term 2 focused on a UX analysis of the company’s website navigation bar.
